## Features & Highlights

- **Context-Aware Task List**: A centralized view of active and pending tickets.
- **Drag & Drop Prioritization**: Easily organize and rank tasks.
- **Always-On-Top Widget**: A compact floating widget that keeps active tasks in focus without consuming screen space.
- **Dark/Light Themes**: Dynamic UI adapting to your visual preferences.
- **Steps & AC Tracking**: Sub-task checklists ensuring all acceptance criteria are met for each ticket.
